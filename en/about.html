01My story · in my words

I never learned to be just one thing.

I'm from Bogotá and an economist by training, but the truth is I collect ways of looking at the world. I studied economics, though the one I truly love is history: I'm fascinated by how the big phenomena — crises, fads, revolutions — explain people better than any model. Now I'm deep in a master's in artificial intelligence, and in between I read philosophy, psychology and anthropology. As a kid I was the quiet one in class, the one who instead of playing took things apart to understand how they worked inside. Once I knocked the home stereo out cold trying to fix it. I didn't fix it — but the question never left me, and it's basically the same one I get paid to answer today: why does this do what it does?

That curiosity has taken me to strange places. I did stand-up twice in front of strangers, and it's one of the most memorable things I've done. And I spent ten days in total silence: brutal, but worth it in the end — I think I came out a slightly better person.

I work with data because I'm obsessed with understanding what's true and what's noise. But if you ask me who I am, I won't talk to you about Excel. Deep down I want something simple to say and hard to achieve: a meaningful life and time to enjoy it. Traveling every birthday, being with the people I love, and having Apolo waiting for me at the door.
