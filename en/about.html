01My story · in my words

I never learned to be just one thing.

I'm from Bogotá and an economist by training, but the truth is I collect ways of looking at the world: economics, history, a master's in AI, and along the way philosophy and anthropology without asking anyone's permission.

As a kid I took apart the home stereo to understand where the music lived. I didn't fix it — but the question never left me, and it's the same one I get paid to answer today: why does this do what it does?

I've done stand‑up twice in front of strangers. That's how I present results: they laugh or they don't — no dashboard to dress it up.
